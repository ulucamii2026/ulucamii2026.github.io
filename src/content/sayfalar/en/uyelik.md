Our mosque is run by the non-profit association **A.D.M.U.C.M.F.** (ASBL). Operating the mosque, religious services, educational activities and the upkeep of the building are the responsibility of this association — and its true owners are its members.

## Why become a member?

Heating, electricity, maintenance and the continuity of our mosque's services are funded by membership dues and donations. Membership is more than a financial contribution:

- **Voice and vote** — you take part directly in decisions at the general assembly.
- **Elect and be elected** — you elect the board and may stand as a candidate.
- **Transparency** — the association's accounts and activities are submitted to the members' scrutiny at the general assembly.
- **Continuity** — regular dues shield the mosque's future from fluctuations in donations.

## Who can become a member?

Anyone **aged 18 or over** who attends the mosque, embraces the association's purpose and agrees to abide by its statutes may apply for membership.

## How to join?

1. **Application form** — filled in at the mosque; you can obtain it from the imam or the board members.
2. **Board decision** — under Belgian association law (CSA), admission is decided by the board of directors and entered in the register of members.
3. **Dues** — after admission, paying the annual dues makes the membership effective.

## How to pay the dues?

The annual amount is set by the association's bodies and announced at the mosque. Two options:

- **Bank transfer** — using the account details on the right; in the message field write *"Dues 2026 – Full name"*.
- **In cash** — at the mosque, against a receipt.

> **Tip:** set up a yearly **standing order** in your banking app — your dues will then be paid automatically every year.

## Funeral Fund membership is separate

Affiliation to the **Funeral, Mutual Aid and Solidarity Fund** of the Diyanet Foundation of Belgium is a **separate** registration from association membership; one does not include the other. For details, see our [Funeral Services](/en/funeral-services/) page.

For any questions about membership and dues, contact the committee — phone numbers are in the right-hand column and on the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.
