## This section is under construction

The **membership section** of our association is currently being built. Once complete, this page will include:

- **Membership application** — who can become a member, the application form and the board’s approval process
- **Dues information** — the annual dues amount, payment methods and a guide to setting up a *standing order* through your banking app
- **Members’ portal** — a private area where our members can securely view their own membership information and dues history

## How to become a member for now

For membership and dues matters, you can contact the **association committee**; the application form is filled in at the mosque. Phone numbers are shown in the box on the right and on the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.

> The launch of the membership system will be announced on the announcements page and on our mosque’s noticeboard.
