**Salih GÖR took office as Social Affairs Counsellor at the Embassy of Türkiye in Brussels on 20 April 2026.** He was appointed by Presidential Decision No. 2026/56 of 13 February 2026.

## Our institutional connection

Ulu Camii in Marche-en-Famenne is affiliated with the Diyanet Foundation of Belgium. The counsellor’s office and the Foundation are institutional points of reference for religious services and community information.

Our association’s local management is presented on the [Board page](/en/board/). Hajj, umrah and other Foundation services are listed under [Diyanet Services](/en/diyanet-services/).

## Education and career

Born in Samsun in 1981, Salih GÖR memorised the Qur’an at the Sokullu Mehmet Paşa Qur’an course in Fatih, Istanbul. He graduated from imam hatip secondary school in 1999 and from a theology faculty in 2004.

- **2001:** began serving as an imam hatip.
- **2014:** completed the 25th programme at Istanbul’s Haseki Higher Religious Specialisation Centre.
- **2016:** became an assistant religious affairs expert.
- **30 October 2019:** appointed Head of the Eurasian Countries Department.
- **20 April 2026:** took up his post in Brussels.

## Official information and services

Follow the [official Diyanet Foundation of Belgium website](https://www.diyanet.be) for institutional announcements. For local classes and mosque activities, [contact Ulu Camii](/en/contact/).

We wish Mr Salih GÖR every success in his work for our community.
