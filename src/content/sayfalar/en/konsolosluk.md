By postal code range, Marche-en-Famenne falls within the jurisdiction of the **T.R. Consulate General in Brussels**, which covers the Brussels Region, Flemish Brabant, Walloon Brabant, Liège, Namur, Hainaut and Luxembourg provinces. All consular procedures — birth, marriage, death, passport, military service, notarial and citizenship matters — are handled here. Office hours are Monday–Friday 09:00–12:00 / 13:00–16:00. Payments are accepted by bank/credit card only (except the military-service-by-payment fee, which is paid in foreign currency).

## Appointment System

All in-person applications to the Consulate General are made by appointment through the official appointment portal **[konsolosluk.gov.tr](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr)**. The appointment system is **free of charge**; there is no need to book an appointment through any intermediary organisation or individual — apply directly through the official site.

Full list of procedure categories: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index) (Birth, Civil Registry, Marriage, Passport, Military Service, Notarial, T.R. Citizenship, Death, Judicial Matters, Land Registry and Cadastre, e-Devlet, Driving Licence, Employment).

> The document lists below provide a general framework; fees and document requirements may change. Always check the relevant procedure on **konsolosluk.gov.tr** before booking an appointment.

## Procedures Available via e-Devlet

Without visiting the Consulate General, some procedures can also be completed via **e-Devlet** (the Turkish e-government portal):

- Birth certificate (for those born in Turkey)
- Military service status document
- Judicial record certificate
- Civil registry record extract
- Turkey entry-exit record certificate (post-2009)
- Land registry record, power-of-attorney information and tax status enquiry

If you do not have an e-Devlet password, a first-time password can be obtained by booking an appointment at the Consulate General. Further information: [turkiye.gov.tr — Ministry of Foreign Affairs Online Consular Procedures](https://turkiye.gov.tr/disisleri-bakanligi-cki-7890)

### Birth Notification and Civil Registration

A notification must be made to the Consulate General within **60 days** of the birth.

**Documents required**
- Passport/ID documents of the mother and father
- Marriage booklet (and Blue Card, if applicable)
- Residence document
- The child’s Belgian-issued birth certificate (acte de naissance / geboorteakte)

**How to apply**
1. For children born within a marriage, the application can also be made **by post**; for children born outside a marriage, an **in-person application by appointment** is required.
2. The appointment is booked via [konsolosluk.gov.tr](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr).
3. The application is made with the required documents; civil registration and, if needed, T.R. Identity Card procedures are carried out together.

Official source: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/ShowProcedure/1](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/ShowProcedure/1)

### Marriage Notification

A marriage performed before a Belgian municipality (a foreign official authority) **must be notified** to the Turkish Consulate General.

**Documents required**
- The original full copy of the marriage record obtained from the municipality (copie intégrale de l’acte de mariage)
- Original birth certificates of both spouses (no older than 3 months)
- Identity/civil registry documents of both spouses and copies
- A single-doctor health report confirming there is no impediment to marriage
- A court decision, if the wife has been previously divorced

**How to apply**
1. The application can be made in person (by appointment) or **by post**.
2. The completed documents are submitted to the Consulate General.
3. At the end of the process a Family Booklet is issued; an International Marriage Record Extract (Form B) can be obtained on request.

For the current fee and document list: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index) → Marriage

## Death and Transfer to Turkey

In the event of the death of a Turkish citizen in Belgium, the process is carried out in two stages: first the local death certificate is obtained on the Belgian side (municipality / état civil), and then the **Funeral Transfer Certificate** is issued by applying to the Consulate General with these documents.

**Documents required**
- The deceased’s identity document and passport
- Marriage booklet (if applicable)
- The death report / death certificate obtained from the Belgian municipality

**How to apply**
1. The death is first notified to the relevant Belgian municipality (see the Marche-en-Famenne municipality below) and a local death certificate is obtained.
2. Death registration can also be carried out at the Consulate General **by post**.
3. If transfer of the deceased to Turkey is requested, a funeral transfer company applies to the Consulate General **in person, by appointment**, with the original required documents; the Consulate General issues the Funeral Transfer Certificate.
4. An International Death Record Extract (Form C) and an estate (inheritance) application can also be made by appointment within the same process.
5. For persons who have been stripped of citizenship or who have transitioned from asylum-seeker status, permission from the Ministry of the Interior may be required; in such cases the process may take longer.

Official source: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/ShowProcedure/8](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/ShowProcedure/8)

**Our mosque’s role:** all washing, shrouding, transfer and burial procedures for the deceased are carried out by the Diyanet Foundation of Belgium Funeral Fund; in the event of a death, the fund is contacted first. Our mosque leads the funeral prayer and announces the death to the congregation; if the funeral prayer is to be held at our mosque, you can reach our imam.

### Power of Attorney and Notarial Procedures

Procedures such as power of attorney, revocation of power of attorney, consent letters, undertakings and contracts, as well as the certification of foreign authority documents and sworn translations, are carried out under the Consulate General’s notarial authority.

**Documents required**
- Original identity document/passport
- The T.R. identity number of the person being granted power of attorney
- For those unable to read or write, 2 witnesses from outside the family

**How to apply**
1. The appointment is booked via [konsolosluk.gov.tr](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr).
2. The application is made in person on the appointment date.
3. If a sworn translation is required, the Consulate General’s list of registered translators can be used: [bruksel-bk.mfa.gov.tr — Sworn Translator List](https://bruksel-bk.mfa.gov.tr/Mission/ShowInfoNote/411645)

For the current fee and document list: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index) → Notarial

### Passport and Identity

Applications for an ordinary passport, a special/service/temporary passport, a new passport following loss, and a T.R. Identity Card (TCKK) are made at the Consulate General by appointment.

**How to apply**
1. The appointment is booked via [konsolosluk.gov.tr](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr).
2. A T.R. Identity Card application can be made together with a birth notification, or as a separate appointment.
3. The application is made in person on the appointment date.

For the current fee and document list: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/Index) → Passport

### Military Service (Paid Exemption)

To benefit from the paid military-service exemption, one must have worked abroad, as an employee or employer, for at least **3 years (1,095 days)** under a residence/work permit (time spent in Turkey is not counted towards this).

**Documents required**
- Residence permit and front/back copies of your identity document
- 2 passport-style photos
- Employment document/payslips and at least 36 months of social-insurance service records

**How to apply**
1. **Mandatory distance-learning training** must be completed before applying: [dovizle.msb.gov.tr](https://dovizle.msb.gov.tr) (registration with T.R. identity number and e-mail).
2. The appointment is booked via [konsolosluk.gov.tr](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr).
3. Unlike other procedures, the paid-exemption fee is paid **in cash**, in foreign currency, on the day of the appointment.

As the current fee changes periodically, no fixed figure is given on this page; for current information see [msb.gov.tr — Paid Military Service Exemption Procedures](https://www.msb.gov.tr/Askeralma/icerik/dovizle-askerlik-islemleri) and [dovizle.msb.gov.tr](https://dovizle.msb.gov.tr)

### Citizenship and the Blue Card

Procedures for acquiring Turkish citizenship, registering multiple citizenship, renouncing citizenship, and the **Blue Card** are handled under this category. The Blue Card is issued to those who were Turkish citizens by birth and who have renounced citizenship with permission under Article 28 of Law No. 5901.

**Documents required (example for the Blue Card)**
- 3 biometric photographs on a white background, taken within the last 6 months
- Original and copy of the foreign citizenship document (certificate/passport)
- Original and copy of the certificate of renunciation of citizenship

**How to apply**
1. The appointment is booked via [konsolosluk.gov.tr](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr).
2. All citizenship procedures require an in-person application by appointment.

Official source: [konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/ShowProcedure/7](https://www.konsolosluk.gov.tr/Procedure/ShowProcedure/7)

## The Belgian Side: Marche-en-Famenne Municipality

Events occurring in Belgium such as births, marriages and deaths are first notified to the **Marche-en-Famenne Municipality (Commune)** civil registry department (état civil); notification to the Turkish Consulate General is made after, or in parallel with, this local procedure.

**Civil Registry Department (État civil)**
- Address: Hôtel de Ville, Boulevard du Midi 22, 6900 Marche-en-Famenne
- Phone: +32 84 32 70 10
- Appointments and online procedures: [eguichet.marche.be](https://eguichet.marche.be) · [marche.guichet-citoyen.be](https://marche.guichet-citoyen.be/demarches/)

**Death notification (décès)**

Every death within the municipal boundaries must be notified to the état civil within **24 hours** of being known. Documents required: the doctor’s death report (model IIIC) and the deceased’s identity documents (identity card, marriage booklet, driving licence, passport, etc.).

Source: [marche.be — Avis de décès](https://www.marche.be/accueil-citoyen/avis-de-deces-9635/) · [marche.be — État civil](https://www.marche.be/administration/les-services-communaux/etat-civil-86/)
